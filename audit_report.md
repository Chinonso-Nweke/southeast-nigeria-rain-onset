# Data trust report

NASA POWER and ERA5 agree weakly at all 5 places. Treat the planting-date numbers as indicative and read the later-start finding as unconfirmed.

## Enugu: weak agreement

- Yearly totals barely agree (correlation 0.08): the two sources disagree on which years were wet or dry.
- The later start differs: +23 days in NASA POWER, +7 days in ERA5.
- The change in yearly rain differs: -21% in NASA POWER, -6% in ERA5.
- Only NASA POWER shows a statistically significant jump in the start date; ERA5 does not.
